A single-line joke about office life — the whole entry. A judge model reads
entries two at a time against a published brief and keeps the funnier one.

> My calendar finally had a free hour, so my boss booked it to discuss my time management.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (88 characters, one line,
no trailing newline). Pure Python stdlib, deterministic: two runs are
byte-identical.

## Why this line

Rubric winner of 7 candidates (24/25): a setup anyone who has worked in an
office recognises (a rare free hour in the calendar), a turn (the boss
spends it on a time-management meeting), the punchline last, and the
surprise is the irony itself — the meeting about time management is the
theft of time.
